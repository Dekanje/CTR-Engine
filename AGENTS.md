Use small isolated changes, independent fixtures, negative tests, fuzz/property tests, holdouts, paired comparisons and uncertainty where relevant. Record commits, toolchain, content hashes, seeds, counts, failures, timing and uncertainty; preserve reproducibility. No invented evidence, silent exclusions or training-only promotion.

Update status, limitations, evidence, decisions, changelog and handover at milestones.
