## Admission decision and legal research

Riot Developer Portal https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/riftbound and https://developer.riotgames.com/policies/riftbound describe restrictions for digital tools including automated rule enforcement. They do not establish that this project's particular private offline research is permitted. The Croatian State Intellectual Property Office explains the distinction between methods and protected expression at https://www.dziv.hr/en/intellectual-property-protection/copyright/copyright-work/protected-by-copyright/ ; WIPO's general explanation is at https://www.wipo.int/en/web/copyright/protection . These sources support distinguishing independently expressed abstract methods from copied expression. They do not settle database rights, contractual terms, applicable jurisdictions or the scope of Riot's digital-tools policy. No case-specific legal determination or third-party permission established. No support ticket required for generic work; none submitted.

Required admission record per source: actual immutable locator, version and effective scope, exact digest, publisher, provenance, permitted use basis and restrictions, reviewer decision. Maintain a separate record for automated activity; a copyright distinction is not a determination of compliance with developer policy. Unresolved sources remain unadmitted. Do not publish official card text, artwork or bulk datasets in this public repository.
